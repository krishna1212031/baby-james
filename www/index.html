How this app works

Hand the device to your child — every tap, drag, or poke anywhere on the screen makes something fun happen. There’s no way to lose or do anything wrong.

To open this menu again later (change settings or quit): press and hold the top-right corner of the screen for about 2 seconds until a small icon appears, then tap that icon 3 times quickly.

Pick your child’s age group to set the right pace, and use Customize… to fine-tune any single game.

Once you’re in this menu, a single tap on that same icon closes the app (with a confirmation first).
