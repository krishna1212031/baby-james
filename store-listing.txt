Gentle touch & drag games for babies and toddlers. Offline, no ads.


FULL DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------
Baby James is a collection of simple, soothing touch games made for babies and toddlers (0–3+ years) to play on their own phone or tablet, safely, with a parent nearby.

There's nothing to win, nothing to lose, and no way to "fail" - every tap, drag, or poke gets a happy response: a balloon pops, a bubble drifts away, a star shoots across the sky, an animal moos or quacks back.

11 GENTLE GAMES
- Balloons - tap to pop, more float up
- Touch the Dot - a pulsing dot that giggles when tapped
- Fireworks - tap twinkling lights to burst them
- Match the Dots - drag a dot onto its matching color
- Drag the Dot - a floating dot you can drag anywhere
- Bubbles - extra soft and slow, great for the youngest
- Peekaboo - tap anywhere for a giggling face
- Finger Trails - drag to paint glowing color trails
- Night Sky Stars - send stars shooting across the sky
- Animal Sounds - tap a cow, dog, cat, or duck
- Musical Keys - tap colorful bars to play notes

MADE TO GROW WITH YOUR CHILD
Every game can be tuned to your child's age, starting from 0-9 months, all the way to 3+ years. Speed, size, and how much is on screen at once can all be adjusted - individually, per game - so it's never too fast or too cluttered for little hands and eyes.

SAFE BY DESIGN
- No ads, ever
- No internet connection needed or used
- No data collected, nothing sent anywhere
- No in-app purchases
- A deliberate two-step gesture (press and hold a corner, then triple-tap a small icon) opens the parent menu, so your child can't stumble into it or exit the app by accident

Made with love for parents who want their baby to have a few minutes of calm, happy screen time without any of the usual worries.
