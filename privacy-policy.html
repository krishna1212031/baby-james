Privacy Policy for Baby James

Last updated: 2026

Baby James is an offline game designed for babies and young children. We take your family's privacy seriously:

  • No data is collected. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or analytics.
  • No internet access is used. The app works entirely offline and does not connect to any server.
  • No advertising. The app contains no ads and no ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • Settings only. The app saves your chosen game settings (such as age group and difficulty sliders) locally on your own device only, using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.
